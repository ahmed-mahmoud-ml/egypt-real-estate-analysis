egypt-real-estate-analysis

## 🎯 Business Questions \& Analytical Objectives
===

# 

# This project analyzes the Egyptian real estate market using listings data collected through web scraping. It answers 10 business questions across four pillars.

# 

# \### 1. Pricing \& Valuation

# 1\. \*\*Geographic Price Benchmarking:\*\* What is the average price per square meter (EGP/sqm) across major cities and districts (e.g., New Cairo vs. Sheikh Zayed vs. New Administrative Capital)?

# 2\. \*\*Key Price Drivers:\*\* Beyond total area, how much do floor level, finishing type (Core \& Shell vs. Fully Finished), and view affect the unit price?

# 3\. \*\*Primary vs. Resale:\*\* Is there a significant price premium or discount between Primary (developer) and Resale units within the same location?

# 

# \### 2. Supply \& Market Composition

# 4\. \*\*Property Type Distribution:\*\* Which property types dominate current listings (Apartments, Villas, Townhouses, Twin Houses, Studios, Duplexes)?

# 5\. \*\*Unit Size \& Bedroom Patterns:\*\* What are the most common unit areas and bedroom counts in each region (e.g., compact investment units vs. family-oriented compounds)?

# 6\. \*\*Share of Active Listings:\*\* Which developers and compounds account for the largest share of active listings?

# 

# \### 3. Payment Plans \& Affordability

# 7\. \*\*Cash vs. Installments:\*\* What is the ratio of listings offered for Cash vs. Installment plans across regions?

# 8\. \*\*Financing Terms:\*\* What are the average down payment percentages and installment durations (in years) in new cities compared to established neighborhoods?

# 9\. \*\*Delivery Status Impact:\*\* How does delivery status (Ready-to-Move vs. Off-Plan / Under Construction) affect unit price and payment flexibility?

# 

# \### 4. Investment \& ROI

# 10\. \*\*Rental Yield:\*\* Which neighborhoods and property types offer the highest estimated Gross Rental Yield, calculated as (annual rent ÷ purchase price) × 100?
